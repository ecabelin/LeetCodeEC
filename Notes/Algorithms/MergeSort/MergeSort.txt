Algorithm Type: Divide & Conquer Sorting
Time Complexity: O(n log n) 
Space Complexity: O(n)

Desc: 
1. Divides input list into two sublists.
   - Till one element
2. Merges pairs of sorted sublists. 
   - Compares smallest elements 
   - Appends them in order
   - Continues till a single sorted array results

Complexity & Efficiency
- A recursive level performs linear-time merges 
  over all elements
- log n levels

Worst Case vs. Average Case
WC == AC 

Variants
- Top-Down Merge Sort
- Bottom-Up Merge Sort 
- External Merge Sort
- Tim Sort

Applications: 
- Database engines
- Linked-list sorting
- External storage 

Sample:
6 5 12 10 9 1
LEFT_1: 6 5 12
LEFT_11: 6
LEFT_12: 5 12
RIGHT_1: 10 9 1
